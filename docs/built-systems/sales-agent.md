# Sales Agent & Floating Presence — Rules

Canonical sources: `src/components/sales/FloatingSalesAgent.tsx` (global voice pod & page awareness), `src/app/layout.tsx`, `src/lib/agora-agent-client.ts`, `src/app/api/agora/session/start/route.ts`.

---

## Rules & Decisions

- **Strict System Separation**: The sales agent module (`src/components/sales/`) must remain completely isolated from the property onboarding studio (`src/components/dashboard/onboarding/` / Elena Vance). Changes, prompts, and brain logic for sales must never alter or create dependencies inside the onboarding system.
- **Cross-Page Voice Call Persistence**: Because `FloatingSalesAgent` is mounted at root layout level (`src/app/layout.tsx`), Agora WebRTC voice sessions remain active and uninterrupted during client-side navigation between public pages (e.g. Home, Listings, Listing Details).
- **Dashboard Exclusion & Unmount**: Sarah is strictly forbidden inside the Owner Dashboard. When navigating to `/dashboard` or any subpage, Sarah is completely unmounted (`return null`), and any active voice session is terminated immediately.
- **Dashboard Navigation Interception**: If a user attempts to navigate to `/dashboard` or its subpages while an Agora voice call is active, navigation is intercepted to display a confirmation prompt:
  - `[Disconnect & Go to Dashboard]`: Disconnects the call (`endCall()`) to stop Agora billing and proceeds to the dashboard.
  - `[Stay Here]`: Cancels navigation and keeps the call active.
- **Call Close & Disconnect Confirmation**: Clicking "Disconnect" or the "X" close button during an active call must prompt: `"Are you sure you want to close this call?"` with `[Yes, End Call]` and `[Keep Talking]`, preventing accidental termination.
- **Microphone Permission Pre-Flight**: Microphone access must be checked via `navigator.mediaDevices.getUserMedia` before `/api/agora/session/start` is dispatched. If permission is denied, the start request is blocked client-side so zero ungrounded or billed Agora sessions are created.
- **Dedicated `sales_agent` Caller Type & "Hi!" Greeting**: The sales agent connects using `callerType: "sales_agent"`, greeting with `"Hi!"` over Agora SD-RTN and powered by Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite (`gemini-3.5-flash-lite`).
- **Contextual Property Search & Sticky City Context**: When callers first ask for properties with specific criteria, Sarah prompts for a city if omitted. Once a city is established in the conversation or displayed on screen, it remains the locked search context: subsequent refinements (e.g. "pet-friendly", "3 BHK", "under 50k", "for rent") query within that city without re-prompting. Cities only change when the caller explicitly requests a different city.
- **Silent Tag, Iterative Refinement & RTM Re-Sync ("Keeping Agora Free")**: Sarah emits silent `[SEARCH:...]` tags (filtered out of TTS via `skip_patterns: [4]`) with parameters for city, pets, bedrooms, budget, or filter resets. Turn-aware deduplication allows Sarah to call search repeatedly across turns. The background search worker queries PostgreSQL and reports results back to Sarah via RTM `[SEARCH_RESULT:]` to prevent Agora gateway audio lag.
- **`[SEARCH_RESULT:]` is sent with APPEND priority, never INTERRUPTED**: the cue arrives while Sarah is still speaking her acknowledgment beat, and INTERRUPTED tells the Cloud Gateway to abandon that sentence and answer immediately — which is what cut her off mid-word. APPEND makes the gateway hold the cue until her current interaction ends. Never queue this client-side off `isAgentSpeaking` instead: that flag is written by five uncoordinated event sources and reads false during the gateway's "thinking" phase, which is exactly when the cue lands.
- **One search per tag, one cue per search**: Sarah's `[SEARCH:]` tag is dispatched only by the hook's `onSearchRequest`. Never re-scan the transcript for the same tag in the component — each extra scan fires another search and another spoken announcement. The speculative search off the caller's own speech exists only to open the hub early and must pass `announce: false`, because Sarah has not acknowledged anything yet.
- **Pure Voice GSAP Search Showcase**: Sarah controls `src/components/sales/GsapSearchHub.tsx`. To demonstrate pure voice AI power, the hub is 100% voice-driven with zero manual buttons, search inputs, or clickable city chips, displaying active voice badges and voice-guided empty states.
- **Live Dialogue Stream & Auto-Scroll**: During an active call, Sarah's pod expands to host `src/components/sales/SalesDialogueStream.tsx`, mirroring the conversational transcript UX (user speech bubbles, Sarah responses with `SA` badge, real-time speaking indicators, and auto-scroll toggle). The display is strictly speech-driven with zero browser speech APIs.
- **Numbered Results & Confirm-Before-Open**: Search cards render a visible `Result N` badge and the `[SEARCH_RESULT:]` cue carries the same numbered list back to Sarah, so "open search result 2" and "open the 4 BHK one" resolve to the same card the caller is looking at. Sarah must read the match back and get a yes **before** opening anything; only then may she emit `[OPEN_PROPERTY:index=N]`, which navigates to that listing and collapses the hub. Opening is **tag-only, with no spoken-language fallback** — unlike every other screen action, it navigates the caller away, so a sentence that merely discusses opening a home must never trigger it.
- **Never let an open request re-run the search**: the speculative user-speech fast-path in `FloatingSalesAgent.tsx` treats "4 BHK" as a refinement. If it fires on "open the 4 BHK one", the results renumber between Sarah's confirmation and the caller's yes, and result 2 opens a different home. Open-intent speech is excluded from that fast-path, and Sarah's prompt bans "checking", "searching", "looking up" and "pulling up" on the confirm and open turns for the same reason — those words match the spoken search fallback.

## Property-Page Mode

- **The prompt is swapped mid-call, never the session**: landing on a listing during an active sales call POSTs `llm.system_messages` to the Agora gateway's agent `/update` endpoint (`updateAgoraAgentPrompt`), so Sarah gains that home's knowledge base without dropping audio or re-billing a session. Send `system_messages` **alone** — the gateway overwrites `llm.params` wholesale, so including it partially erases the model name and silently downgrades the brain. Both entry paths build their prompt from `property-agent-context.ts`, so a cold start and a handover describe the same home identically.
- **`/update` is silent, so the `[PROPERTY_OPENED:]` cue is what makes her speak**: swapping the prompt produces no turn. The cue must be sent *after* the update resolves and with `append` priority — sent first, she answers it using the search prompt; sent as `interrupted`, it cuts her off (see the APPEND rule above).
- **The fit check is computed in code and handed to her as fact** (`property-fit.ts`): an LLM comparing a budget against an asking price and a floor price will occasionally flip the comparison, and on a live sales call a flipped comparison is a promise the owner never authorised. Sarah never works out whether a home suits the caller — she receives the verdict and sells around it.
- **Three miss severities, because "no" is rarely the honest answer**: `adjustable` (inside the owner's authorised range — trade a concession for a listed condition, never gift one), `bridgeable` (below the floor but within 10% of it — stop negotiating and invite them to see the home and meet the owner), `hard` (no version of the deal exists — name the blocker and help them search instead). The band is measured from the floor price, not the asking price.
- **The owner's floor price is never disclosed, quoted, or confirmed to exist.** It shapes the verdict and never enters the prompt: `PropertyPromptFacts` has no floor field, and the miss detail strings carry no numbers. A test asserts the floor cannot appear in the built prompt.
- **A requirement the caller never stated is `unknown`, never `missed`.** Unknowns are what the 2–3 discovery questions ask about, ordered so *this home's own constraints* come first — a no-pets building asks about pets before the caller falls for the place. Re-asking anything already recorded is banned: it tells the caller she was not listening.
- **Only a stated prohibition is a refusal**: an absent pet policy is `unknown` and offered for owner confirmation, never an invented "no". Same contract as the `NOT_SPECIFIED` rule — the agent says a detail is unverified rather than guessing it.
- **Property mode keeps the search tags.** "Let's find you something better" must be an action she can take, so `SEARCH_CAPABILITY_INSTRUCTIONS` is shared verbatim between both prompts. This is also why no reverse handover exists: she stays in property mode after the caller leaves the page and simply retargets on the next one.
- **Honest framing only, enforced in the prompt**: no invented urgency or other-buyer interest, no fabricated deadlines, no shaming a caller for hesitating or for their budget, no claim absent from the verified knowledge base. Sell the life they would live there, and quote the *measured* walk and drive times from `kbData.nearbyDistances` rather than "close to" or "great connectivity".
- **Call memory is two threads, and only one of them is prose** (`sales-journey.ts`): `requirements` is merged field by field and **never re-summarised**, because re-summarising prose on each visit is lossy and "under 50,000" decays into "budget-conscious" — which is the exact number the fit check must be precise about. `visits` is appended to, one entry per home keyed by slug. Only the per-visit notes are model-generated, and only from the turns since the last update.
- **One request carries both the memory update and the prompt swap.** A single navigation fires both, and as two independent calls they land in either order — the notes for the home being left must be folded in *before* the prompt for the home being opened is built from them. A visit note's title is resolved server-side from the slug, never trusted from the browser.
- **The user-speech search fast-path is silent on a listing page**: "does it have three bedrooms?" is a question about the home on screen, and answering it with a search drops the hub's overlay over that home. Sarah's own `[SEARCH:]` tag still works there — that is the deliberate pivot path.

### Roadmap seam: three-way owner call

Not built. The planned next step dials the owner who listed the home into the live call at the two moments the prompt's `ESCALATION` section already names: a caller stuck just below the floor, and a question the knowledge base cannot answer. Deliberately **no** tag, owner phone field, or client plumbing exists for it yet — when it lands it becomes a third escalation bullet and a tag handled the way `[OPEN_PROPERTY:]` is. The seam is documented in `sarah-property-prompt.ts`; do not scaffold it early.
